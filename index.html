Welcome to Georg Bökman's webpage, last updated 2026-10-20.

I'm a computer scientist focusing on computer vision and machine learning. My main interests are in geometric deep learning, including both geometry for deep learning (e.g. equivariant neural networks) and deep learning for geometry (e.g. learning-based methods for 3D reconstruction from images).

Currently, I'm a postdoc in the Amsterdam Machine Learning Lab working with Erik J. Bekkers.

I completed my PhD at Chalmers in 2024 under the supervision of Fredrik Kahl, and co-advisors Michael Felsberg and Axel Flinth.
